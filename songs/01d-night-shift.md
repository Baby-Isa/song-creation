# Song 1, take 4: "Night Shift" (style 1, the lyricist crew, subtler)

## Zafar on take 3, 30 Oct: a keeper
Catchy hook without being pop; genuinely good lyrics. Two notes:
1. The horns, chimes and bells clashed, a bit like a kids-TV jingle.
2. Too on the nose: the song says what it's doing ("three pens, nobody's the star") and names every night worker. Let the listener do some work.

What changed:
- Sound: the vibraphone is gone, and chimes, bells, horns and brass are excluded. Just a dusty Rhodes and upright-bass loop with a low piano stab.
- Lyrics: the workers are hinted at, never named (blue scrubs → "wearing blue like a promise she kept"; the cabbie → "the rank", "the meter"; the cleaner → "gloss on a desk").
  The competition is shown, not said (lines that cap each other, a bridge where they finish each other's lines). "Night shift" only appears in the hook.
- Kept: the hook, the loop, the three voices, the best lines.
