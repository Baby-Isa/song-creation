# Lala, Little Ee-sa

**Style:** [00 Car songs](../styles/00-car-songs.md), its calm version. **Status:** version 2, written, not yet made.

**Brief (Zafar, 30 Oct 2026):** a bedtime song, sleepy from the start, using "lala" for sleep.

**Style prompt (the base, about 30% slower and gentler; Zafar, 30 Oct 2026: the 72 BPM lullaby was too slow):**
```
Gentle electronic baby-pop, 115 BPM, warm E-flat major, soft bouncy 4/4, about 2 minutes. Warm, soft sing-song female lead, gentle family backing vocals and humming. Plucky synths, toy piano, glockenspiel, music box, soft handclaps, round mellow bass, light brushed drums. Sweet sung "lala" hooks, gentle "ba-ba" and "boop" hooks, soft rising pitch slides. Calm, cosy, warm, gentle mix, never shrill, winding down softly at the end.
```

**Exclude styles:**
```
trap hi-hats, autotune, distorted guitar, EDM drop, screaming, loud, dark, minor key, sad
```

**Notes:** "Lala salama" is Swahili for "sleep peacefully". "Bismillah" is said at bedtime.

**Lyrics:**
```
[Intro | soft music box, humming]
Mmm-mmm... mmm-mmm...
Lala... lala...

[Verse 1 | soft and slow]
The sun has tucked into its bed
The moon pops up its sleepy head
The bees buzz home, the fishies doze
The kitty curls up, nose to toes

[Chorus | gentle and swaying]
Lala, lala, little Ee-sa
Lala, lala, off to sleep
Lala, lala, dreams are waiting
Soft as clouds and twice as deep

[Verse 2 | softer]
Your little fingers, one by one
Have waved goodbye to all the fun
Your little toes have had their play
Now tiptoe dreams have come to stay

[Chorus | gentle and swaying]
Lala, lala, little Ee-sa
Lala, lala, off to sleep
Lala, lala, dreams are waiting
Soft as clouds and twice as deep

[Bridge | humming, whisper soft]
Mmm... the stars are winking, one, two, three
They're keeping watch for you and me
Bismillah, we're right here
Shh... shh... nothing to fear

[Final Chorus | softer and slower]
Lala, lala, little Ee-sa
Lala salama, off to sleep
Lala, lala, dreams are waiting
Soft as clouds and twice as deep

[Outro | humming, fading to silence]
Lala... lala...
Mmm...
Goodnight, Ee-sa...
```
