**Brief (Zafar, 30 Oct 2026):** a bedtime song, sleepy from the start (not bouncy), using "lala" for sleep.

Gentle lullaby, 70 BPM, warm E-flat major, soft swaying 6/8, about 2 minutes. Soft, breathy, warm female lead, hushed humming and gentle family harmonies. Music box, kalimba, soft felt piano, warm pads, light brushed shaker, slow round bass. Sweet sung "lala" hooks, slow falling melodies, getting quieter and slower towards the end. **Notes:**
- **This song has its own calm style, not the bouncy base** (Zafar, 30 Oct 2026).
- "Lala salama" is Swahili for "sleep peacefully". "Lala kahle" is Zulu for "sleep well", written "Lala ga-shleh" so Suno gets close to it. **Ask his mum to check that one.**
- "Bismillah, close your eyes" is a bedtime habit. No names of people.
- Gentle sung sounds only: a hum, "shh", a purr, a yawn, a kiss.
