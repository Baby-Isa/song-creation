# Time to Pray

**Style:** [00 Car songs](../styles/00-car-songs.md). **Status:** written, not yet made.

**Brief (Zafar, 30 Oct 2026):** a fun, bouncy prayer song, not too serious. Wudhu (splashing water), the musallah, counting the tasbih, and the everyday words: Bismillah, Alhamdulillah, SubhanAllah. No names of people (no Prophet, no Imams).

**Style prompt (the base, plus the prayer flavour line):**
```
Bouncy electronic baby-pop, 163 BPM, bright E-flat major, 4/4, about 2 minutes. Warm sing-song female lead, gentle family backing vocals, giggly ad-libs and call-and-response. Plucky synths, toy piano, glockenspiel, handclaps, round bouncy bass, snare rolls into each chorus. Crisp "ba-ba", "boop" and "pop" vocal hooks, sung animal noises and silly sound ad-libs, rising pitch slides, key change before the last chorus. Warm, clean, gentle mix, never shrill. Warm daf frame drum and soft hand percussion.
```

**Exclude styles:**
```
trap hi-hats, autotune, distorted guitar, EDM drop, screaming, dark, minor key, sad
```

**Notes:**
- The wudhu follows the Shia order: face, right arm, left arm, then wipe the head and wipe the feet.
- The tasbih lines go in the tasbih's order (Allahu Akbar, Alhamdulillah, SubhanAllah) without naming anyone.
- The sneeze is followed by "Alhamdulillah", which is real manners and was babies' favourite sound in the research.
- One Zulu word: "Yeh-boh!" (yes).
- The turbah is left out, because Zafar didn't mention it.

**Lyrics:**
```
[Intro | bouncy hand drums]
Ee-sa! Ready to pray?
Yeh-boh! Hooray, hooray!
Bismillah... here we go!
Ba-ba-boom!

[Verse 1 | splashy and light]
Roll up sleeves and turn the tap
Splish! Splash! Clap, clap, clap!
Water on the face, so cool
Right arm, left arm, that's the rule
Wipe the head and wipe the feet
Drip, drop! Fresh and neat!

[Chorus | big and bouncy, everyone sings]
Splish, splash, wudhu time!
Clean and fresh and feeling fine!
Roll the musallah, one, two, three
Ee-sa, Ee-sa, pray with me!

[Post-Chorus | call-and-response]
Alhamdulillah! (Alhamdulillah!)
Alhamdulillah! Hooray, hooray!

[Verse 2 | bouncy]
Tasbih beads go tick, tick, tick
Round and round, not too quick
Little fingers, one by one
Counting, counting, so much fun!

[Tasbih | gentle call-and-response]
Allahu Akbar! (Allahu Akbar!)
Alhamdulillah! (Alhamdulillah!)
SubhanAllah! (SubhanAllah!)
Tick, tick, tick, what a happy sound!

[Chorus | big and bouncy, everyone sings]
Splish, splash, wudhu time!
Clean and fresh and feeling fine!
Roll the musallah, one, two, three
Ee-sa, Ee-sa, pray with me!

[Verse 3 | playful call-and-response]
Ah... ah... A-CHOO! (Alhamdulillah!)
Yummy dinner? (Bismillah!)
Look at Ee-sa! (Mashallah!)
Every day we say... (Alhamdulillah!)

[Bridge | soft and slow]
Hands up high, then bow down low
Head on the ground, nice and slow
Quiet heart and a smiling face
SubhanAllah, in every place

[Final Chorus | key change up, everyone sings]
Splish, splash, wudhu time!
Clean and fresh and feeling fine!
Fold the musallah, one, two, three
Ee-sa, Ee-sa, prayed with me!

[Post-Chorus | call-and-response]
Alhamdulillah! (Alhamdulillah!)
Alhamdulillah! Hooray, hooray!

[Outro | soft and smiling]
Ameen!
Hee-hee!
Mwah!
```
