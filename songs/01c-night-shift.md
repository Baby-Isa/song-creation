# Song 1, take 3: "Night Shift" (style 1, the lyricist crew)

## Zafar on take 2, 30 Oct
Much better; likes it, doesn't love it. Liked the repeating hook and the repeating sample loop, and the concept.
But it sounds like the later era: a room of hype men posturing, with no substance.
He wants it dialled back 5-10 years: lyricists with a notepad, a crew out-doing each other with clever bars, more of the jazz roots.

What changed:
- Three rappers pass the mic, each with a distinct voice. Friendly competition, no hype ad-libs, no posturing (the "heater" line is gone).
- Substance: the night shift is the city's night workers (driver, nurse, baker, cabbie, cleaner, guard) and the writers working alongside them.
- More jazz: one repeating upright-bass and Rhodes/vibes loop runs the whole track; lighter, swung drums.
- Kept: the hook (one line changed), the late-night mood, and his favourite clever lines from take 2.
