# Ee-sa-saurus

**Style:** [00 Car songs](../styles/00-car-songs.md). **Status:** to redo: too samey (Zafar, 30 Oct 2026). Not made.

**Brief (Zafar, 30 Oct 2026):** back to the time of the dinosaurs, with Isa as a little Stegosaurus going through the Jurassic world. A friendly T-rex.

**Style prompt:**
```
Bouncy electronic baby-pop, 163 BPM, bright E-flat major, 4/4, about 2 minutes. Warm sing-song female lead, gentle family backing vocals, giggly ad-libs and call-and-response. Plucky synths, toy piano, glockenspiel, handclaps, round bouncy bass, snare rolls into each chorus. Crisp "ba-ba", "boop" and "pop" vocal hooks, sung animal noises and silly sound ad-libs, rising pitch slides, key change before the last chorus. Warm, clean, gentle mix, never shrill. Big stompy drums, tuba bass and playful brass stabs.
```

**Exclude styles:**
```
trap hi-hats, autotune, distorted guitar, EDM drop, screaming, dark, minor key, sad
```

**Notes:**
- The hook is "Ee-sa-saurus", his name turned into a dinosaur.
- Every dinosaur is friendly. The T-rex's joke is his tiny arms, which can't clap.
- Sung sounds throughout: stomp, rawr, munch, flap, whee, boom.
- One Swahili word (twende) and one Zulu word (yebo).
