# You Are My Stegosaurus

**Style:** [00 Car songs](../styles/00-car-songs.md). **Status:** version 2, written, not yet made.

**Brief (Zafar, 30 Oct 2026):** a mum describing the adventures her stegosaurus child will have in the big dinosaur world, and the dinosaurs he'll meet. Include the line "You are my stegosaurus".

**Style prompt:**
```
Bouncy electronic baby-pop, 163 BPM, bright E-flat major, 4/4, about 2 minutes. Warm sing-song female lead, gentle family backing vocals, giggly ad-libs and call-and-response. Plucky synths, toy piano, glockenspiel, handclaps, round bouncy bass, snare rolls into each chorus. Crisp "ba-ba", "boop" and "pop" vocal hooks, sung animal noises and silly sound ad-libs, rising pitch slides, key change before the last chorus. Warm, clean, gentle mix, never shrill. Big stompy drums and playful tuba bass.
```

**Exclude styles:**
```
trap hi-hats, autotune, distorted guitar, EDM drop, screaming, dark, minor key, sad
```
