# Haa-naa, Meri Jalebi

**Style:** [15 Bollywood pop duet](../styles/15-bollywood-pop-duet.md). **Status:** written, not yet made.

**Brief (Zafar, 3 Nov 2026):** Song A of the Zafar and Hana story, the chase, in the "You Are My Soniya" style. It covers London and America, the calls till dawn, no → "I'll think about it" → yes in two weeks, the sorority queen who's a jalebi inside, the laid-back funny businessman, gifts and poems, and the nervous first meeting. It must be under 4 minutes. The full brief is in `research/zafar-hana-story.md`.

**What the Hinglish means (and where the story is hidden):**
- *London mein baarish, raat ke do baje / dil ke taar baje*: rain in London at 2 a.m.; her laugh on the phone makes his heartstrings play.
- *Saat samundar door... tu hai meri galli mein*: seven seas away, yet it feels like she's on his street.
- *Jaise baadal barse*: "he talks like clouds pouring". It rhymes with Ab-barse and is a dig at the chatty, funny businessman.
- *Sorority ki president, campus ki shaan / rakho dhyaan*: "the pride of campus; the queue is long, mister, mind yourself."
- *Meri subah ho gayi / meri abhi raat hai*: "my morning has come / it's still night for me". These are the all-night calls across time zones.
- *Uljhi uljhi, meethi meethi... andar chaashni*: "all twists and knots, sweet sweet... the world calls you tough, but I know it's syrup inside". The jalebi is the highly strung, hard-shelled, soft-centred Hana.
- *Thodi teekhi, zyada meethi*: a little sharp, mostly sweet.
- *Shaadi karogi? / No! / Sochungi / Do hafte mein ho gaya ikraar*: "Will you marry me? / No! / I'll think about it / within two weeks, the promise was made."
- *Teen lafz kehne the... gaya main bhool / udd gaye hosh*: at the first meeting he forgot the three words; seeing her, he lost his senses.
- *Kal bolega? / Kal pakka! / Dekhenge!*: "You'll say it tomorrow? / Tomorrow, for sure! / We'll see!" This is the cliffhanger into Song B, where he says it on day two.
