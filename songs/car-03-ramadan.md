# Ramadan, Ramadan

**Style:** [00 Car songs](../styles/00-car-songs.md). **Status:** version 3, written, not yet made.

**Brief (Zafar, 30 Oct 2026):** fasting all day in Ramadan, iftar coming soon, getting the plates and bowls out, charity and helping others, saying Alhamdulillah when we're thankful. No names of people. Use "daku" for the pre-dawn meal, with no sand, labbayk or hajj. Version 3 is simple, on the base style (version 2's taarab went too far). The family says "Ramadan Mubarak".

**Style prompt:**
```
Bouncy electronic baby-pop, 163 BPM, bright E-flat major, 4/4, about 2 minutes. Warm sing-song female lead, gentle family backing vocals, giggly ad-libs and call-and-response. Plucky synths, toy piano, glockenspiel, handclaps, round bouncy bass, snare rolls into each chorus. Crisp "ba-ba", "boop" and "pop" vocal hooks, sung animal noises and silly sound ad-libs, rising pitch slides, key change before the last chorus. Warm, clean, gentle mix, never shrill. Warm hand drums and extra handclaps.
```

**Exclude styles:**
```
trap hi-hats, autotune, distorted guitar, EDM drop, screaming, dark, minor key, sad
```
