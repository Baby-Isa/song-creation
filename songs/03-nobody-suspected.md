# Song 3: "Nobody Suspected the Baron" (style 9, DOOM-style eccentric)

Picked because it's the furthest from "Night Shift" while staying in Zafar's top plays (King Geedorah). A fresh start; only `song-rules.md` carries over.

How this group differs from group 1:
- **One character, not a crew:** the Baron, a comic-book villain in a dented helmet, told in the third person. An original character, not an imitation.
- **The sound:** warped vintage samples (a detuned organ, cartoon strings) and loops that start in odd places, lo-fi, deadpan and slightly behind the beat.
- **No sung chorus:** the hook is a warped vocal sample from an old radio drama, repeated. Narrator skits between the verses.
- **The wordplay:** dense multi-syllable rhymes that run across lines, absurd food-and-household detail.
- **The hidden layer, never said outright:** the Baron's "evil schemes" are small secret kindnesses, and the mask holds the feelings in rather than hiding him.
