# Song 1, take 2: "Night Shift" (style 1, Tribe and Gang Starr)

## Zafar on take 1 ("Second Cup"), 30 Oct
Too poppy and too old-school big band, and too soft: it read like kids' rhymes, not 90s rappers.
Even the smooth ones have a cutting edge. Parts sounded like electro swing, which he likes, but as its own group.

What caused it, and the fix:
- The prompt said "bright, mellow, playful, chantable call-and-response, muted trumpet". Now: minor key, hard drums, chopped piano, no horns.
- Tea, slippers and sugar cubes made it twee. Now: night, the basement, hunger, quiet confidence, punchlines that cut.
- The chanted hook made it pop. Now: a half-spoken hook over DJ scratches, Premier-style.
