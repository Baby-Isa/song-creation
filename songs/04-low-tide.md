# Song 4: "Low Tide" (style 4, cool-cat jazz lounge)

Why this group: it's distinct from both "Night Shift" (a crew trading punchlines over a sampled loop) and the Baron (one weird character),
and it's the most musical group, after Zafar's note that musicality is what made him keep the Baron's take.

How this group differs:
- **A live jazz band feel** rather than a sampled loop: a walking upright bass, brushed drums, finger snaps, a muted sax and flute.
- **A man and a woman trading verses**, in a cool, poetic, beatnik delivery: more poetry and imagery than punchlines.
- **The hook is a murmured two-voice refrain** over the bass walk.
- **The hidden layer, never said outright:** staying cool and unhurried while everything rushes; patience wears down stone.
