**Style of Music**
```
early 90s East Coast jazz rap, 90 BPM, one repeating dusty sampled loop of upright bass and warm jazz piano with soft Rhodes, runs the whole track, laid-back swung boom-bap drums, crisp not heavy, vinyl crackle. Three male rappers passing the mic: a smooth, unhurried lead who sits back on the beat and pauses after his punchlines to let them land, a deeper playful second voice, equally deliberate, and a fast intricate third. Witty, understated, confident, never rushed. The hook is half-sung by a lighter, airy, more modern voice floating over the loop, a fresh contrast to the verses, with light DJ scratches. Short instrumental break where the loop is filtered, then drops back in. Warm late-night mood, vocals clear and forward.
```
