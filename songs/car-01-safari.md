# Safari

**Style:** [00 Car songs](../styles/00-car-songs.md). **Status:** written, not yet made.

**Brief (Zafar, 30 Oct 2026):** a "back to Africa" animal song for our son: the family has African heritage. We get in the Jeep, go on safari, see the giants, savannah, jungle, the African night sky, then home we go. More African instruments.

**Style prompt (the base, plus the safari flavour line):**
```
Bouncy electronic baby-pop, 163 BPM, bright E-flat major, 4/4, about 2 minutes. Warm sing-song female lead, gentle family backing vocals, real toddler giggles and call-and-response. Plucky synths, toy piano, glockenspiel, handclaps, round bouncy bass, snare rolls into each chorus. Crisp "ba-ba", "boop" and "pop" vocal hooks, tiny pauses for surprise sounds, rising pitch slides, key change before the last chorus. Warm, clean, gentle mix, never shrill. East African flavour: marimba, kalimba, djembe, shakers, bright highlife guitar.
```

**Exclude styles:**
```
trap hi-hats, autotune, distorted guitar, EDM drop, screaming, dark, minor key, sad
```

**Swahili used:** jambo (hello), twende (let's go), twiga (giraffe), tembo (elephant), kiboko (hippo), nyota (stars), kwaheri (goodbye). Each is spelled the way it sounds, with the English nearby.

**Lyrics:**

[Intro | jeep engine starts, baby giggles]

Beep! Beep!
Ready? Ready?
Jam-bo!

[Verse 1 | bouncy and light]
Sun's up early, the sky's so wide
Hop in the jeep for a bumpy ride
Long grass going swish, swish, sway
Who's that peeking out to play?

[Break | leaves munching, birds chirping]
Munch, munch! Way up high!
Twee-gah's nose is in the sky!
Long neck, long legs, spots and all
Hello, giraffe, you're SO tall!

[Chorus | big and bouncy, handclaps]

Africa, Africa, shout JAM-BO!

[Verse 2 | drums get bigger]
Stomp, stomp, here they come
Giants marching like a big bass drum
Big ears flap and long trunks swing
Tem-bo, tem-bo, what do you sing?

[Break | elephant trumpet, ground thumps]
Paa-paaa! Paa-paaa!
Boom! Boom! Boom!

Into the jungle, green and deep
Ooh-ooh! Aah-aah! Monkeys leap!
Drip, drop, pitter-pat

Pink and gold on the mountain's crown
Ki-li-man-ja-ro, sleepy and high

[Final Chorus | key change up, everyone sings]
Safari, safari! Beep, beep, beep!
Bumpity, bumpity, home in the jeep!
Twen-deh, twen-deh! Home we go!
Africa, Africa, we love you so!

[Outro | engine fades, giggles]
Brrrm... brrrm...
Beep! Beep!
Kwa-heh-ree!
Hee-hee!
