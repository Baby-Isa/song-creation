# Zafar and Hana: the story brief (Zafar, 3 Nov 2026)

Source material for the two K3G-style songs. State it outright or hint at it, the way the reference songs do.

**Pronunciation, as spelled for Suno:** Hana is "Haa-naa". Zafar Abbas is "Zuff-arr Ab-barse".

- **An ocean apart:** Zafar lived in London and Hana in America.
- **The phone calls:** they talked for hours, all night, until dawn.
- **The proposal:** at first she said **no**, then **"I'll think about it"**, and then, after only two weeks, **"I will marry you"**. This was before they had ever met in person.
- **The courtship:** they sent each other gifts and wrote each other poems and stories.
- **Hard times:** they faced difficulties and challenges, overcame them together and came out stronger.
- **Flights:** she flew to him and he flew to her. They had golden days together, then long stretches apart again.
- **Meeting in person:** on the first day he was too nervous to say "I love you". On the second day he said it, and she said it back.
- **The wedding:** he flew over with his whole family and married her in her own home. It was a very blessed wedding.
- **Zafar:** a businessman, laid-back and funny.
- **Hana:** president of her sorority, very popular, a bit highly strung, confident and tough on the outside. Underneath she's a **jalebi**: crisp outside, all twists, full of syrup inside. (It replaces the original "cupcake with sprinkles".)
