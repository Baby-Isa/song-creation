## Proposed style groups (13)

Each group: what defines it, a recipe, and its songs.

1. **Tribe bounce: the core.** Upright or Rhodes bass, crisp swung drums at 92-98 BPM, warm jazz chords, a witty conversational flow, a chantable hook. Recipe: *bright jazz loop + bouncy bass + playful call-and-response*. Songs: Tribe ×7 (The Hop sits in group 2), Brand Nubian, Grand Puba, Large Pro (In the Sun, I Juswanna Chill), Black Star (Astronomy, Brown Skin Lady), Hieroglyphics, The Roots.
2. **Dilla swing.** Off-grid, drunken swing; soft, wistful samples; warm and reflective. Recipe: *loose unquantised drums + filtered soul/bossa sample + gentle flow*. Songs: Pharcyde (Runnin', Drop, Moment In Time), Slum Village, Illa J, The Hop.
3. **West coast jazz-play.** Mellow loops, quick intricate flows, crew energy, humour. Recipe: *dreamy loop + fast clever rhymes + a sung or group hook*. Songs: Souls of Mischief ×3, Passin' Me By, People Under The Stairs.
4. **Cool-cat jazz lounge.** Live or near-live jazz, very relaxed, poetic slang, beatnik cool. Recipe: *live upright bass + brushed drums + horn or vibes + a whispered, cool flow*. Songs: Digable Planets ×3, Camp Lo, Bahamadia, Jungle Brothers, Fugees Nappy Heads.
5. **Jazzmatazz: rap with a real jazz band.** Named soloists (trumpet, vibes), jazz-funk grooves, less rap and more band. Songs: Guru ×5, House of Pain/Guru remix.
6. **Lyrical wordplay showcase.** The lyrics are the point: punchlines, internal rhyme, tongue-twisters, over a jazzy boom bap. Recipe: *simple loop, rap front and centre, a rhyme pattern you notice*. Songs: Common Invocation, J-Live, Big L Put It On, Lord Finesse, Day One, Rakim, Binary Star, Coops That Jazz.
7. **Soulful NY reflection.** Pete Rock/Premier-produced, a piano or horn loop, serious storytelling. Songs: Nas ×4, AZ, Pete Rock & InI, Gang Starr ×2, Jeru ×2, Masta Ace, Talib Kweli, Thieves In The Night, KRS-One.
8. **Dark hardcore NY.** Murky, menacing, minor key. The furthest from the target. Songs: Mobb Deep ×2, Black Moon, Big Pun, Big L/2Pac, Artifacts.
9. **DOOM-style eccentric.** Cartoonish samples, odd loops, playful villain persona, collage. Songs: King Geedorah ×2, Viktor Vaughn, Babies & Fools.
10. **Modern jazz-beats (the "modern kick").** Today's production of the 90s sound: cleaner low end, lo-fi texture, sometimes live players. Songs: FloFilz & Blu, CoryaYo, kamau, Elements of Music, Nujabes ×2.
11. **New UK jazz rap.** London/Dublin voices, nu-jazz musicians, soft conversational flow, nostalgic. **Your current favourites.** Songs: Coops, Rejjie Snow & Loyle Carner 1992, 2nd Exit/Alfa Mist, Egyptian Luvr.
12. **Downtempo, trip-hop and instrumental jazz-funk.** Little or no rap; groove-first. Songs: Rae & Christian, Aim, Medeski Martin & Wood, Kraak & Smaak, Love Speakeasy, Take A Look, Let Off A Couple.
13. **Outliers.** One-offs: Benga (dubstep), Guru & Damian Marley (reggae), Zealots (eerie doo-wop).
