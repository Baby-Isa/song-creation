# Suno guide

What we know about Suno, and what we've learned from our own takes. "Check" means it's unverified: confirm it in the app and update this file.

## Limits (from guides found online, 30 Oct 2026; check in the app)

- **Style field:** about 1,000 characters on v4.5 and later. Suno may cut off longer prompts without warning, so put the most important tags first.
- **Lyrics field:** about 5,000 characters.
- **Exclude styles:** a separate box for sounds to avoid (for example "trap hi-hats, EDM drop").
- **Latest model:** one site reports v6 (Sept 2026). Check which model the account uses.
- **Artist and song names:** don't use them. Describe the sound instead: era, instruments, drums, vocal delivery, texture.

Sources: [HookGenius: Suno character limits](https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-character-limits/), [HookGenius: v6 guide](https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-v6-guide/).

## Lyrics conventions

- Put structure tags on their own line in square brackets: `[Intro]`, `[Verse 1]`, `[Pre-Chorus]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Bridge]`, `[Rap Verse]`, `[Outro]`.
- Brackets can also carry performance notes, for example `[Female vocal]`, `[Male vocal]`, `[Duet]`, `[Scratch break]` or `[Beat drops out]`. Check how reliably each one works.
- Text in brackets is not sung. Text in round brackets is usually sung as backing vocals or ad-libs.
