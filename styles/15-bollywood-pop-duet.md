# Style 15: Bollywood pop duet (2001 film, "Soniya" style)

**Songs in this group:** "You Are My Soniya" (K3G, 2001). The analysis is in `research/k3g-reference-analysis.md`.

**What they share:**
- Glossy early-2000s Bollywood pop with a Western club and R&B finish, at a mid-tempo, hip-sway groove built for a hook step.
- A flirty male and female duet: he woos, she teases.
- Hinglish lyrics, and a hook that joins an English phrase to an endearment.
- Light Indian colour on top of the pop, not underneath it.
